About Undefeated: Gridiron Legends

Undefeated: Gridiron Legends is a free browser game where you draft the greatest football players of all time onto a single roster, simulate a 20-game season, and see if you can go undefeated.

Every mode starts the same way: build a full roster from a pool of all-time-great players across eras, positions, and teams, then let the season play out. A new Daily Challenge roster is available every day, or play Classic, Offense Only, or Two-Minute Drill any time.

Why we built it

We wanted a quick, replayable way to argue the "greatest of all time" debate that football fans have every offseason — instead of just talking about it, draft your answer and let a simulated season decide if your roster actually holds up.

Game modes

  • Daily Challenge — the same roster pool for everyone, once a day.
  • Classic — full stat readouts as you build your roster.
  • Offense Only — a leaner, offense-heavy roster build.
  • Two-Minute Drill — a fast, clock-racing spin mode.
