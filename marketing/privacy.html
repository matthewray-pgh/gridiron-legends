Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Undefeated: Gridiron Legends ("we," "our," "the game") collects when you visit undefeatedgridironlegends.com or play the game, and how that information is used.

Who we are

Undefeated: Gridiron Legends is an independently developed football roster-drafting game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL, NFLPA, or any NFL team.

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. Depending on which features you use, this may include:

  • Gameplay data stored on your device — your roster, streaks, and progress are saved locally in your browser and are never sent to us.
  • Analytics — aggregate, non-identifying information such as page views, general location (country/region level), device/browser type, and referring site, collected via Google Analytics, which uses cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions.
  • Advertising data — if advertising is enabled on the site, our advertising partner(s), including Google AdSense, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visit to this and other sites. See "Advertising" below.

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or payment information through the game itself.

Cookies

We do not use cookies for core gameplay. Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions (see "Analytics" below), and if advertising is active on the site, our ad partners may set their own cookies, described below.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand overall site traffic (such as how many people visit and which pages are popular). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as page views, approximate location (country/region level), device/browser type, and referring site. This data is used in aggregate to understand traffic patterns and is not used by us to identify you individually.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all sites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by blocking cookies as described above.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can clear locally stored gameplay data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads via the Google link above.
  • Most browsers let you block cookies entirely; doing so may affect ad display but not core gameplay.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the Contact page.
